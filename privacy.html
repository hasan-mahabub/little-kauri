Little Kauri Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Little Kauri is a learning game made by Hasan for families. This policy explains how the app handles information about children and grown ups.

Information stored on your device

Little Kauri stores player names, colour themes, chosen characters and toys, learning progress, rewards, and speech preferences in the app's local storage on your device. The app does not send this profile information to Hasan or to a Little Kauri server. The Android app disables Android app data backup.

There are no user accounts, advertisements, analytics, or tracking tools in Little Kauri.

Speech and voice data

Spoken instructions use a voice installed on the device. Little Kauri offers locally available voices so the app can speak without sending the text to an online voice service. The child's profile name may be spoken aloud on the device. The app does not record or store speech or audio from the microphone.

If a grown up chooses to install speech voice data, Android opens the device's text to speech service to manage that download. Little Kauri does not receive or store the downloaded voice data.

Sounds and other app content

The letter sound recordings are bundled with the app and play locally. Little Kauri does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or phone information.

Keeping or deleting information

Player profiles and progress remain on this device until a grown up deletes a player or resets that player's progress in Parent Corner. Speech voice preferences are separate device settings and can be reset in Parent Corner. Uninstalling the Android app removes its locally stored profiles and progress. Browser progress is stored separately in that browser and can be cleared by deleting the browser's site data.

Children and grown ups

Little Kauri is designed for children starting school and is intended to be used with a parent or caregiver. A grown up can create and delete player profiles and can choose the speech voice used by the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Hasan using the developer contact email shown on Little Kauri's Google Play listing.
