App name
Little Kauri

Short description
Playful early learning games for phonics, numbers, and school readiness.

Full description
Play, talk, read and count with Little Kauri, a playful learning companion for children getting ready for school.

Little Kauri offers short activities inspired by the New Zealand Ministry of Education guide First 6 months at school:

• Practise letter sounds with locally stored recordings from the Ministry's Sounds and letters cards.
• Explore counting, number order, counting backwards, addition, subtraction, patterns, measurement and shapes.
• Listen to stories, sound out simple words and practise early sight words.
• Trace letters, numbers and a child's name.
• Build speaking and listening confidence with questions, instructions and open ended talk prompts.
• Earn stars, stickers and toys while practising at a child's own pace.

Create separate child profiles on the device. Learning progress stays on that device. Little Kauri has no ads, online accounts, analytics, or in app purchases. Spoken instructions use downloaded voices available on the device. Some voices must be installed in Android text to speech settings before use.

Little Kauri was made for family fun by Hasan for his daughter Manha. It is an independent app and is not an official Ministry of Education app or endorsed by the Ministry.
